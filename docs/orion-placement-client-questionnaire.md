### E. Terms

#### 19. How do you charge?
> We operate exclusively on a non-exclusive, contingency-based model:  
> - **Standard fee:** 23% of the candidate's compensation, as defined in our agreement.  
> - **No upfront fees.** Clients only pay when they successfully hire our candidates.  
> - **No exclusivity requirements:** Employers can recruit internally and work with other agencies simultaneously.

#### 20. What happens if a placement leaves early?
> We offer a standard **90-day replacement guarantee**. If a candidate leaves during that period, we work to identify a qualified replacement according to our agreement at no additional charge.

#### 21. When is the fee invoiced and due? Do you require exclusivity?
> - **Invoiced:** On the candidate's first day of employment.  
> - **Due:** 15 days thereafter.  
> - **Exclusivity:** Agreements are non-exclusive. Clients may recruit internally and engage other agencies simultaneously.

### F. How You Screen

#### 22. What screening checks do you perform before presenting candidates?
> Our screening process is tailored to the position and candidate:  
> - For candidates who clearly meet the requirements, we may conduct abbreviated screening to expedite the interview process.  
> - For candidates requiring further evaluation, we conduct additional screening through questionnaires, phone conversations, and qualification assessments to verify their experience, suitability, and compensation expectations.  
> - Background checks can be coordinated upon request during the offer stage.

#### 23. Where do your candidates come from, and how large is your active legal pool?
> We maintain an active legal candidate pool of **over 100,000 professionals**.  
> Our sourcing strategy includes:  
> - Hundreds of job boards and resume databases.  
> - LinkedIn recruiting and direct outreach.  
> - Company websites and publicly available professional information.  
> - Phone calls, voicemail drops, email campaigns, and text messaging.  
> - Direct communication through recruiting platforms and job boards.  
> *We engage both active job seekers and passive candidates who aren't currently looking for new opportunities.*

#### 24. What information do you include with each candidate submission?
> Our submissions typically include:  
> - Candidate resume.  
> - Summary notes and qualifications.  
> - Explanation of why the candidate fits the role.  
> - Compensation expectations.  
> - Any additional information requested by the client.  
> *We customize submissions to each client's requirements and hiring preferences.*
